### 🚀 Featured Projects

#### 🌊 [OceanEmbed](https://github.com/manasmmm/notlike) — Smart India Hackathon 2026 (SIH26066) · Team Crisis Workers
Reconstructs **3-D subsurface ocean temperature** (15 depths, down to 1000 m) across the North Indian Ocean using **only surface satellite data**: SST, salinity, sea-level height, currents and winds.
- Harmonises multi-mission satellite feeds onto a daily 0.25° grid
- XGBoost module turns each location's surface history into a compact temporal embedding
- Graph-based model over (lat, lon, depth) nodes reconstructs full depth profiles
- Validated against real **ARGO float** measurements

`Python` `XGBoost` `Graph ML` `Satellite Oceanography` `Jupyter`

#### 🛰️ [PAM-ROFI](https://github.com/manasmmm/PAM-ROFI) — Irrigation Advisory Engine
Fuses Sentinel-1 radar and Sentinel-2 optical data on Google Earth Engine to map crops (sugarcane, paddy, wheat), track crop moisture stress by growth stage, and generate 8-day irrigation priority maps for Meerut, Western UP.
`Google Earth Engine` `Random Forest` `SAR` `geemap`
